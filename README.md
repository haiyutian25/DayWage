DayWage

A simple daily work hours and wage tracker.

DayWage is an open-source app designed for hourly workers, part-time workers, and daily wage workers.

Easily record your daily working hours, work shifts, overtime, and weekends, then automatically calculate your earnings.

Features

🕒 Record daily working hours

☀️ Track day shifts and night shifts

💰 Automatically calculate daily wages

📅 Track weekend and overtime earnings

📊 View daily, weekly, and monthly earnings

📝 Keep a personal work and income history

Work your hours. Track your earnings.
